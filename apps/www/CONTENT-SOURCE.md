## About us.docx

> Numeric Technologies is a Ghana-based IT solutions company
> specializing in the customization and integration of software for the
> pharmaceutical and healthcare sector. We combine deep regulatory
> domain expertise with hands-on software development to help
> pharmaceutical manufacturers, distributors, and pharmacies build the
> systems they need to operate safely, efficiently, and in full
> compliance with FDA Ghana requirements.
>
> Our work today spans regulatory and quality compliance services —
> GMP/QMS gap assessments, audit readiness, and documentation systems —
> alongside IT support and systems integration for the ERPs, POS, and
> inventory platforms that keep pharmaceutical distribution running.
> Every engagement feeds directly into our long-term mission: building a
> dedicated Quality Management System (QMS) and Laboratory Information
> Management System (LIMS) platform, designed to integrate with FDA
> Ghana's digital infrastructure and support both the regulator and
> pharmaceutical companies in meeting the highest international
> standards.
>
> We see ourselves as more than a vendor. As Ghana works toward
> advancing its WHO regulatory maturity from Level 3 to Level 4, and as
> global pharmaceutical companies look to enter West Africa through the
> AfCFTA, we aim to be the trusted local technical partner that makes
> that growth possible — de-risking compliance, strengthening local
> capability, and connecting international expertise with African
> market opportunity.

## Services.pdf

**Advisory & Strategy** — Expert guidance to navigate Ghana's
regulatory landscape and plan market entry with confidence.
- Regulatory strategy & product classification
- Market entry & compliance risk assessment
- FDA Ghana submission management (GFIRMS)
- Regulatory intelligence & policy monitoring

**Quality Systems** — End-to-end quality infrastructure that keeps you
audit-ready, always.
- QMS design & implementation
- GMP gap assessments & mock audits
- SOP development & document control
- CAPA & deviation management

**Digital & Technology** — Purpose-built platforms that digitize
compliance and connect you to regulatory systems.
- QMS & LIMS platform deployment
- FDA system integration & data connectivity
- Inventory, expiry & traceability systems
- Custom pharma software development

**Safety & Trials** — Comprehensive support across clinical development
and post-market safety.
- Clinical trial operations & TMF management
- Pharmacovigilance & adverse event reporting
- Signal detection & risk management
- Periodic safety update reports (PSURs)
