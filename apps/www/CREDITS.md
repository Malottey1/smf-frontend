## What was rejected

- A studio-backdrop photo from the same Lagos series (30678211,
  "Professional Man Working on Laptop") — same fake-backdrop issue as
  the Team photo, without even the "Team" section's excuse for using it.
- A "diverse professional team" photo that turned out to be shot in
  Greenville, South Carolina, USA — despite surfacing on an "African
  professionals" search. Confirmed the location in the photo's own
  metadata before rejecting it.
- Several "Black woman lawyer / office" results from European stock
  studios (Kaboompics and similar) — diverse casting in a European
  studio isn't African context, which is what the brief asks for.

## Search notes

Location+industry search terms (e.g. "Ghana pharmacy") mostly return
generic global stock, not Ghana-specific results. Widening to "African"
+ industry terms, and later to the Ninthgrid photographer specifically
(a Lagos-based studio whose stated focus is "diverse Afrocentric visual
assets"), surfaced everything usable above. Nothing on this site is
Ghana-specific; everything is at least genuinely West/Central African
in origin, and every location claim in an alt text or caption is
accurate to the photo's actual metadata.
