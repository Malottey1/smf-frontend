NUMERIC TECHNOLOGIES — IDENTITY REVIEW

Three monogram concepts, three palette directions

For sign-off before any page of the corporate site is built. Monogram source files live at packages/brand-corporate/concepts/; palette hex values are verified against WCAG AA below, not assumed.

Monogram concepts

Each is built on a 32-unit grid, angles limited to 90°/45°, one consistent stroke weight per mark. Shown at full size, at favicon scale (14 / 18 / 24px), and reversed on a dark ground.

CONCEPT A

Checked N

14–24px
reversed

A geometric N with one facet cut into the diagonal — a single angled notch standing in for a checkpoint, without literally drawing a checkmark icon.

CONCEPT B

Joined Forms

14–24px
reversed

Two independent L-brackets, not touching, that read as one mark only because the eye closes the central gap — the clearest visual argument for "two systems meeting," and the one built to hold a genuine two-colour split (each bracket its own tone).

CONCEPT C

Modular N

14–24px
reversed

A pixel-grid N — discrete square modules rather than continuous strokes — the most literal expression of "systematic, modular construction," and the one most likely to read as software rather than as a services firm.

Palette directions

Structure only, not Pfizer's hues: one deep authoritative tone, one warm energetic tone used structurally (never as small body text), a warm neutral, a near-black. Every pairing below is measured, not eyeballed — ratios use the WCAG relative-luminance formula.

Reading the tables

"Bright on white" failing AA in all three directions is expected and matches the brief: the energetic tone is for headers, rules and small structural accents — never small body copy on a white ground. Every direction's deep/ink/neutral combination clears AAA comfortably, which is the pairing that carries all actual reading.

Typefaces used on this page (Archivo for display, Source Sans 3 for body) are the two candidates named in the brief, shown here at working size rather than described — both will be self-hosted as woff2 in the real build, this page only borrows Google Fonts for review speed.
